+Ultra: free gym workout tracker for iPhone and Apple Watch

Lifting log, strength coach and calorie counter

+Ultra (say “Plus Ultra”) is a free workout tracker and gym log for iPhone and Apple Watch. It logs your sets, plans your next session with a built-in strength coach, and tracks calories and macros. There is no subscription, no account and no ads, and your log is stored on your iPhone.

Coming soon to the App Store

+Ultra at a glance

  • Price: free. No subscription, no ads, no in-app purchases that unlock anything; optional one-time tips only.
  • Account: none needed.
  • Devices: iPhone (iOS 17 or later) and Apple Watch (watchOS 10 or later). Not available for iPad or Android.
  • Where your data lives: on your iPhone, with an optional backup to your own iCloud.
  • Exercises: 260+ built in, plus your own.
  • Programs: 12, including StrongLifts 5×5, Push/Pull/Legs, Upper/Lower and a full-body starter.
  • Apple Health: two-way sync for workouts, food, water, weigh-ins, sleep, HRV and resting heart rate.
  • Switching apps: imports a CSV export from Strong or Hevy, or your Apple Health workouts.

Log strength workouts fast

Pick from 260+ exercises or add your own. Log reps, weight and effort, with warm-up, failure and drop sets. On iOS 26 or later, say “100 for 6” and the set is logged. A rest timer runs on your phone, Lock Screen and Apple Watch, and a plate calculator knows your gym’s plates.

A strength coach that plans your next session

+Ultra suggests your next weight, spots plateaus and flags muscles you’re undertraining. With Apple Health connected it compares your sleep, HRV and resting heart rate with your own usual and holds an increase when you look less recovered. Tell it your goal and how many days you can train, and it picks a program with progressive overload built in. With Apple Intelligence, the coach explains its plan using Apple’s on-device model.

Personal records and progress you can see

Every PR is celebrated with context, like “that’s one and a half times your bodyweight.” Stats show training and weight by week, month, 3 months or year, with a consistency calendar, records, estimated one-rep max, volume per muscle, strength levels and milestones. Share a Training Report PDF with a coach or physio.

Calorie counter and macro tracker

Search foods, scan a barcode or nutrition label, photograph your plate, or describe a meal in your own words. Calorie and macro targets adapt to your weigh-ins. Water and weigh-ins too. It is free, like the rest of the app.

Apple Watch, Siri and widgets

Start a session on your wrist, log sets and see your heart rate between them. Start a workout, log a set or ask “Am I recovered?” with Siri. Widgets, Control Center and Live Activities keep today’s plan and your rest timer a glance away.

Private by design

Your log lives on your iPhone. Only this leaves it: a backup to your own iCloud, food searches to Open Food Facts with nothing that identifies you, your weekly totals on the friends leaderboard once you join, your PRs if you share them, and anything you share. Read exactly what goes where in the privacy policy.

Common questions

Is +Ultra really free?

Yes. Workouts, the coach, stats, calorie tracking, Apple Watch and the leaderboard are all free, with no subscription and no ads. Optional tips don’t unlock anything.

Does +Ultra need an account?

No. There is no sign-up. The friends leaderboard uses your iCloud account only if you choose to join it.

Can I move my workout history from another app?

Yes. Export a CSV from Strong or Hevy and import it during setup or in You > Data & Backup. +Ultra shows a preview first.

Does it work without Apple Health or Apple Watch?

Yes. Both are optional. Connecting Apple Health lets the coach use your sleep and heart data to judge recovery.
