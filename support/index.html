Frequently asked questions

Is my data private?

Yes. +Ultra has no account and no ads, and keeps your workouts, food, measurements and photos on your iPhone. Only these leave it: a backup to your own private iCloud if you turn it on; food searches and barcodes sent to Open Food Facts, with nothing that identifies you; your weekly totals and a display name (“Anonymous” unless you choose to show yours) on the friends leaderboard once you open it; your best set for each built-in lift, only if you turn on “Share my PRs with friends”; and anything you share yourself. Apple Intelligence and voice features run on your iPhone. Full details are in the privacy policy.

Does +Ultra work with Apple Health?

Yes, both ways, and only with your permission. +Ultra saves the workouts you log (with effort), and the food, water and weigh-ins you log, to Apple Health, and updates or removes its copy when you edit or delete one. It reads workouts from other apps and how hard they were, steps, active energy, weigh-ins, sleep, heart rate variability, resting heart rate and the times of mindful sessions (to leave out heart rate variability read during Breathe), so your stats stay complete and the coach can ease off when you look less recovered. Sleep also fills the Sleep page (Stats > All Charts > Sleep, the Coach’s recovery sheet, or the Sleep card if you pin it to Home): last night’s stages, a Sleep Score that is the app’s own estimate, how regular your bedtimes are and the week’s sleep debt, worked out on your iPhone and never saved. If you turn on Cycle-aware insights (You > Data & Backup), it also reads your period days. You > Apple Health shows exactly what goes each way. Change access any time in the Health app (tap your picture, then Apps, then +Ultra) or in Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > +Ultra.

How do I back up my data?

Go to You > Data & Backup and tap Back Up to iCloud. After that, +Ultra keeps the backup up to date after each workout. Restore from iCloud and Import Data are on the same screen. The backup holds the workouts, food, water and weigh-ins you log, your own exercises, saved meals, gyms and pain notes. Progress photos stay on the iPhone only.

How do I export or delete my data?

You > Data & Backup > Export Data writes a CSV of your workouts, food, water, weigh-ins, your own exercises, saved meals, gyms and pain notes. Import Data reads one back. Delete All Data, at the bottom of the same screen, erases everything +Ultra stores on the iPhone in one go: workouts, routines, meals, water, weight, body measurements, progress photos, form-check videos, pain notes, gyms, friends and your profile. It asks first and tells you exactly what it removes, and it can remove your leaderboard record in the same step. Anything already written to Apple Health stays in Health, where you can delete it. Your iCloud backup stays until you remove it in iOS Settings > your name > iCloud.

Is +Ultra free?

Yes, all of it: workouts, the coach, stats, food and calorie tracking, progress photos, Apple Watch and the leaderboard. There is no subscription, no ads and no account.

How can I support +Ultra?

If you'd like to, You > Support +Ultra has optional one-time tips, paid through the App Store. A tip doesn't unlock anything and never renews, and the app never asks you for one. Rating +Ultra (You > Help & About > Rate +Ultra) helps too.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later and Apple Watch with watchOS 10 or later, plus Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, Live Activities and Control Center controls. Some features need newer software: Control Center controls need iOS 18, and logging by voice needs iOS 26. The coach’s written notes and chat use Apple Intelligence where your iPhone supports it and it is turned on, and otherwise answer from the app’s own rules. +Ultra isn’t available for iPad.

Where are Friends and the leaderboard?

On the You tab, under Leaderboard & Friends; it needs you to be signed in to iCloud. Tap the Friends button there to see your friend code and share it, or to add a friend by theirs. “Show my name” decides whether friends see your name or “Anonymous”, and “Remove me from the shared leaderboard” deletes your published record. Swipe a friend to Remove or Report them.

What are Friends’ PRs?

Friends’ PRs, on the Friends screen, shows each friend’s best set for the built-in lifts. It is off until you turn on “Share my PRs with friends”, which publishes your best weight and reps for each built-in lift, and the day, under your friend code. They come from the workouts you log in +Ultra; nothing from Apple Health is shared. Turning the switch off deletes your shared PRs.

What reminders can +Ultra send?

Turn each one on in You > Reminders: a workout reminder on your training days, one at your usual training time if nothing is logged yet, a week streak at risk, a weekly summary of last week, and food and water checks (the food and protein check needs Calorie Tracking). Health changes, also there and off until you turn it on, sends at most one note a day when your sleep, heart rate variability or resting heart rate stays away from your usual; the note says only “A recovery hint from Apple Health” and what the plan does, and the details are in the app. Reminders never show sleep or any Health reading.

Can I bring my history from Strong or Hevy?

Yes. Export a CSV from Strong or Hevy, then choose it in You > Data & Backup > Import Data, or during setup. +Ultra shows a preview before importing anything.

How do I log a set by voice?

During a workout, tap the mic and say the set, for example “100 for 6”. Speech is turned into text on your iPhone and isn’t recorded. This needs iOS 26 or later.

Can I lock my progress photos?

Yes. In You > Progress Photos, turn on Lock with Face ID (or your passcode). Photos never leave your iPhone unless you share or save one.

How do I send my training to a coach or physio?

Share Training Report, in You > Data & Backup or behind the report button at the top of Stats, makes a PDF of 4, 8 or 12 weeks that you can send from the share sheet.
